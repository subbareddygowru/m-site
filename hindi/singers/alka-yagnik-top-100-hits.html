Alka Yagnik

Alka Yagnik's breakthrough came with "Ek Do Teen" from Tezaab (1988), reportedly recorded while she had a fever, and it kicked off a run of seven Filmfare Awards for Best Female Playback Singer, a record she shares only with Asha Bhosle. Guinness World Records named her the most-streamed artist on YouTube for three straight years running, 2020 through 2022, ahead of Taylor Swift and BTS. This collection gathers her biggest Hindi hits across a four-decade career that shaped the sound of 90s Bollywood.
