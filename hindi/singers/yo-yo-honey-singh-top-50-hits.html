Yo Yo Honey Singh

Honey Singh basically dragged Punjabi rap into the Bollywood mainstream by himself with "Brown Rang" and International Villager back in 2011 — before that it barely existed on the radio. He went quiet for a stretch after opening up about a bipolar diagnosis, then came back swinging with "Makhna," which is one of Indian pop's better comeback stories. Party anthems only, from Lungi Dance to Blue Eyes.
