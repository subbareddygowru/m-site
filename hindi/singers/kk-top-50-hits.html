KK

KK sang an estimated 3,500 advertising jingles before he ever got a shot at playback singing, and it shows in the sheer polish of his 1999 debut album Pal, whose title track and "Yaaron" have become a fixture of school farewells ever since. "Tadap Tadap Ke" from Hum Dil De Chuke Sanam later earned him his first Filmfare nomination, kicking off two decades as one of Bollywood's most reliably emotive voices. This playlist gathers the songs that made his warm, unshowy delivery so widely loved.
