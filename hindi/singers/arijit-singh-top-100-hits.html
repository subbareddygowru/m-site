Arijit Singh

Before he was the most-streamed artist on Spotify India seven years running, Arijit Singh was working behind the scenes as a music programmer and assistant to composer Pritam. His 2013 breakthrough "Tum Hi Ho" from Aashiqui 2 changed that overnight, and by 2025 he'd matched Kishore Kumar's record of eight Filmfare Awards for Best Male Playback Singer. This playlist gathers the hits that made him modern Bollywood's default voice for heartbreak and longing.
