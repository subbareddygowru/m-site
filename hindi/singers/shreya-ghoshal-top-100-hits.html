Shreya Ghoshal

Shreya Ghoshal made her Bollywood debut at just 16 with Sanjay Leela Bhansali's Devdas (2002), and the film's "Bairi Piya" won her a National Film Award before she'd even finished her teens. Since then she's picked up five National Film Awards and ten Filmfare Awards South, sung in more than a dozen Indian languages, and become the singer most senior playback legends point to as the best of her generation. This collection gathers the Hindi hits that built that reputation, from Devdas-era classical numbers to modern chartbusters like Chikni Chameli.
