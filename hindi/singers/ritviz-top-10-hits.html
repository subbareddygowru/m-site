Ritviz

Ritviz grew up listening to his mother's Hindustani classical riyaaz every morning before eventually channeling that training into electronic music instead of the classical stage. His 2017 single "Udd Gaye," made through the Bacardi House Party Sessions talent contest, became an overnight party anthem and launched him as one of India's most streamed independent artists. This playlist collects the tracks where his classical roots and electronic instincts meet.
