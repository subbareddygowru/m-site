About Music Directors

Bollywood music owes its identity to a lineage of brilliant composers. This collection gathers Spotify playlists from Hindi cinema's most influential music directors, covering decades of chartbusters, evergreen scores and dance anthems.

Three composers who shaped Hindi film music

A good film song often belongs as much to the composer as to the singer. This page features A. R. Rahman, whose work blends orchestral depth with electronic textures, Pritam, who has a gift for melodies that stick on the first listen, and Himesh Reshammiya, known for a distinctive, high-energy sound.

Listening by music director is a good way to notice the difference between styles even when the singers and actors change. Choose a composer, play the playlist through, and see which sound feels most like you.
