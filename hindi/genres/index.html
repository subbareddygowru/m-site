Mashups, item songs and remixes

Not every song fits neatly under a singer or a film, and this page is for the ones that do not. The mashup playlist stitches familiar tunes together, the item songs list gathers the high-energy dance numbers that Hindi cinema is famous for, and the remix playlist updates older favourites with a modern beat.

These are mostly party and workout playlists, so the tempo stays up. They also work well when you are cooking, cleaning or setting the mood before guests arrive.
