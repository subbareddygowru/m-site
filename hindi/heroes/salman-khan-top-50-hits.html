Salman Khan

Since debuting with Maine Pyaar Kiya in 1989, Salman Khan has built one of Bollywood's most recognisable song legacies — the kind built more on vibe and swagger than vocal range. Hum Aapke Hain Koun turned "Didi Tera Devar Deewana" into a wedding-season fixture that's never really gone out of rotation, and later on he started lending his own voice to tracks like "Jag Ghoomeya" and "Main Hoon Hero Tera." Say what you want about the acting, the man knows how to pick a song people will dance to for years.

Who Is Salman Khan?

Born Abdul Rashid Salim Salman Khan on 27 December 1965 in Indore, he's the son of screenwriter Salim Khan, one half of the legendary Salim-Javed writing duo. He debuted with a supporting role in "Biwi Ho To Aisi" (1988), but it was Sooraj Barjatya's "Maine Pyaar Kiya" (1989) that made him an overnight star and won him the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut — the film's soundtrack, and his "Prem" persona, became a cultural reset for Bollywood romance.

His song legacy since then has leaned less on his own singing and more on picking soundtracks people can't stop dancing to — "Didi Tera Devar Deewana" from "Hum Aapke Hain Koun..!" (1994) turned into a permanent wedding-season fixture, and after his early-2000s box-office lull he reset his image entirely with "Wanted" (2009) and the "Dabangg" franchise. He's since lent his own voice to tracks like "Jag Ghoomeya" and "Main Hoon Hero Tera." Off screen, he founded the Being Human Foundation in 2007 and has won two National Film Awards as a producer, for "Chillar Party" and "Bajrangi Bhaijaan."

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Salman Khan's breakout song?

"Dil Deewana," from his 1989 debut hit "Maine Pyaar Kiya," established the soft, romantic "Prem" image that defined his early career.

Does Salman Khan sing his own songs?

Occasionally — he's lent his own voice to tracks like "Jag Ghoomeya" (Sultan) and "Main Hoon Hero Tera" (Hero), though most of his soundtracks are sung by professional playback singers.

What is Salman Khan's most iconic wedding song?

"Didi Tera Devar Deewana," from "Hum Aapke Hain Koun..!" (1994), remains one of Bollywood's most-played wedding anthems three decades on.
