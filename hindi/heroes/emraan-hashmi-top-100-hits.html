Emraan Hashmi

Emraan Hashmi carved out a lane nobody else in Bollywood really occupied — moody, obsessive love songs for films that weren't afraid to be a little dark about romance. Somewhere along the way the "serial kisser" tag turned into a genuine reputation for picking soundtracks with real staying power, from Murder to Aashiqui to Jannat and beyond. This one's for anyone who wants the heartbreak-and-longing side of Hindi film music without the usual gloss.
