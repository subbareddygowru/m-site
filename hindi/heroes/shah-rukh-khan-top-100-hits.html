Who Is Shah Rukh Khan?

Born Shahrukh Khan on 2 November 1965 in New Delhi, he trained in theatre under Barry John and studied at Hansraj College before breaking into television with "Fauji" (1989) and "Circus." His Bollywood debut came with "Deewana" (1992), which won him the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut. He was first known for villainous roles in "Baazigar" (1993), "Darr" (1993), and "Anjaam" (1994) — "Baazigar" brought his first Filmfare Best Actor win — before "Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge" (1995) reset his image entirely and made his on-screen pairing with Kajol iconic.

He's since starred in over 100 films, holding a record eight Filmfare Best Actor awards, and won the National Film Award for Best Actor for "Jawan" in 2025. He co-owns the production house Red Chillies Entertainment with wife Gauri Khan, and became a co-owner of the IPL's Kolkata Knight Riders in 2008. He received India's Padma Shri in 2005, France's Ordre des Arts et des Lettres in 2007, and the Legion of Honour in 2014.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Shah Rukh Khan's debut Bollywood film?

"Deewana" (1992), which won him the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut and launched his film career after a run of television roles.

How many Filmfare Awards has Shah Rukh Khan won?

He holds a record eight Filmfare Best Actor awards, part of 14 Filmfare wins overall, and won the National Film Award for Best Actor for "Jawan" in 2025.

What is Shah Rukh Khan's most iconic song?

"Tujhe Dekha To," from "Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge" (1995), is widely considered his defining romantic anthem and remains one of Bollywood's most-loved love songs.
