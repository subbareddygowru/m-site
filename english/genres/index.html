Every genre has its own energy. This collection organizes English songs by genre, so you can jump straight into the exact sound you're in the mood for.

Pick an English genre and start listening

Genre is often the quickest way to find what you want to hear, so this page keeps the English collection simple: hip-hop, rock and classical, each with its own playlist. If you like rap, there is a list of top rappers and a separate one for newer rap releases. Rock fans get a playlist of the genre's most played songs, and the classical page is there for study sessions, long drives or quiet evenings.

Every playlist is hand-picked rather than generated, which means the order and the song choice are meant to feel like a proper listen from start to finish.
