Linkin Park — In Memory of Chester Bennington

Chester Bennington was born on March 20, 1976, in Phoenix, Arizona, and became the voice of Linkin Park in 1999. His extraordinary range, moving effortlessly between soaring clean vocals and raw, guttural screams, gave the band its unmistakable emotional edge. Chester was open about a difficult childhood and a long struggle with addiction, and he poured that pain, honesty and resilience into every song he sang, turning personal struggle into music that resonated with millions of listeners around the world.

From the breakout success of Hybrid Theory in 2000 to the introspective, more melodic sound of One More Light in 2017, Chester's voice carried Linkin Park through nearly two decades of evolution. He passed away on July 20, 2017, at the age of 41. This playlist is dedicated to his memory and includes only Linkin Park songs recorded and released while Chester was alive — a collection that celebrates the voice, the honesty and the artistry he gave the world.
