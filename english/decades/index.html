About Decades

Every decade has its own sound. This collection organizes English songs by era, so you can revisit the classics or catch up on the latest hits, all in one place.

English music, one era at a time

If a song takes you straight back to a particular summer, the decade playlists are the place to start. One collects hits from the seventies, eighties and nineties in a single run, another is a large all-time list, and a third is an oldies-but-goodies playlist for the songs your parents and grandparents still hum.

For something more recent there is an English top hits 2023 playlist, plus a top 1000 list for the people who want a long, varied listen. They work well as background music at home, in the car, or at a get-together where everyone wants something familiar.
