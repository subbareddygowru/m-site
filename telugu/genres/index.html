Every genre has its own flavor. This collection organizes Telugu songs by genre, so whether you love traditional folk roots or high-energy DJ folk remixes, there's a playlist ready for you.

Folk, DJ folk and remix

Telugu folk songs have a strong and loyal audience, and this page gathers them by style. The folk playlist keeps to the traditional sound, the DJ folk list puts the same tunes over heavy beats for dancing, and the remix collection reworks well-known songs for a new generation.

They are good picks for festivals, weddings and road trips, or any time you want something louder than a melody playlist.
