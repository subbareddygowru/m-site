About this Playlist

To put this together, I went through the complete soundtrack of every Telugu film that came out in 2013, not just the songs that were pushed hard on radio and TV at the time. That's how the underrated tracks ended up here — songs that were good enough to belong on this list but never got the attention they deserved. The list of films used as reference for 2013 releases came from Wikipedia's List of Telugu films of 2013 and FilmiBeat's Telugu movies by year: 2013 archive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was the biggest Telugu film of 2013?

"Attarintiki Daredi" — Pawan Kalyan's family entertainer became the highest-grossing Telugu film of all time on release, winning five Nandi Awards and four Filmfare Awards South.

Does this playlist include underrated songs, or just the popular hits?

Both. This playlist was curated by going through every Telugu film released in 2013, using Wikipedia's "List of Telugu films of 2013" and FilmiBeat's movies-by-year archive as reference, so a number of underrated tracks that didn't get their moment sit alongside the well-known hits.

How many Telugu films were released in 2013?

Around 100 straight Telugu films were released in 2013, according to Wikipedia's List of Telugu films of 2013.
