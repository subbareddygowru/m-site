About this Playlist

Compiling this playlist meant sitting through the complete soundtrack of every Telugu film released in 2011, not just skimming the songs that got the most airplay. That's where the underrated picks come from — tracks that deserved more attention than they got when the movie first released. The list of films used as reference for 2011 releases came from Wikipedia's List of Telugu films of 2011 and FilmiBeat's Telugu movies by year: 2011 archive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was the biggest Telugu film of 2011?

"Dookudu" — Mahesh Babu's action comedy was the year's highest grosser and swept Best Film, Best Actor, and Best Music Director at the South Indian International Movie Awards.

Does this playlist include underrated songs, or just the popular hits?

Both. This playlist was curated by going through every Telugu film released in 2011, using Wikipedia's "List of Telugu films of 2011" and FilmiBeat's movies-by-year archive as reference, so a number of underrated tracks that didn't get their moment sit alongside the well-known hits.

How many Telugu films were released in 2011?

Around 105 straight Telugu films were released in 2011, according to Wikipedia's List of Telugu films of 2011.
