About this Playlist

This one came together after going through the soundtrack of every Telugu movie that released in 2012, song by song. A lot of "best of the year" lists just recycle the songs that were already everywhere — this playlist tries to also surface the ones that got missed the first time around. The list of films used as reference for 2012 releases came from Wikipedia's List of Telugu films of 2012 and FilmiBeat's Telugu movies by year: 2012 archive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was the biggest Telugu film of 2012?

"Gabbar Singh" — Pawan Kalyan's remake of "Dabangg" became the year's highest-grossing Telugu film, while S.S. Rajamouli's "Eega" won widespread critical acclaim for its inventive premise.

Does this playlist include underrated songs, or just the popular hits?

Both. This playlist was curated by going through every Telugu film released in 2012, using Wikipedia's "List of Telugu films of 2012" and FilmiBeat's movies-by-year archive as reference, so a number of underrated tracks that didn't get their moment sit alongside the well-known hits.

How many Telugu films were released in 2012?
