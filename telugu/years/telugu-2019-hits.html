Frequently Asked Questions

What was the biggest Telugu film of 2019?

"Maharshi" — Mahesh Babu's drama was among the year's top earners and won the National Film Award for Best Popular Film, though "Sye Raa Narasimha Reddy" and "Dear Comrade" were also major hits that year.

Does this playlist include underrated songs, or just the popular hits?

Both. This playlist was curated by going through every Telugu film released in 2019, using Wikipedia's "List of Telugu films of 2019" and FilmiBeat's movies-by-year archive as reference, so a number of underrated tracks that didn't get their moment sit alongside the well-known hits.

over 200 Telugu films were released in 2019, according to Wikipedia's List of Telugu films of 2019.
