About this Playlist

This playlist is the result of going through every Telugu movie released in 2016 and listening to the soundtrack in full, not just skimming for the songs that were already popular. A few underrated tracks earned their place here alongside the bigger hits, simply because they were too good to leave out. The list of films used as reference for 2016 releases came from Wikipedia's List of Telugu films of 2016 and FilmiBeat's Telugu movies by year: 2016 archive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was the biggest Telugu film of 2016?

"Janatha Garage" — N.T. Rama Rao Jr.'s film topped the year's box office and won six Nandi Awards, more than any other film that year.

Does this playlist include underrated songs, or just the popular hits?

Both. This playlist was curated by going through every Telugu film released in 2016, using Wikipedia's "List of Telugu films of 2016" and FilmiBeat's movies-by-year archive as reference, so a number of underrated tracks that didn't get their moment sit alongside the well-known hits.

How many Telugu films were released in 2016?

Tollywood released well over 200 films in 2016, according to Wikipedia's List of Telugu films of 2016.
