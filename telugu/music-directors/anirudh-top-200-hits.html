Who Is Anirudh Ravichander?

Born on 16 October 1990 in Chennai, Anirudh Ravichander is the son of actor Ravi Raghavendra. He broke out in Tamil cinema with his 2012 debut "3," whose song "Why This Kolaveri Di" became a global viral sensation, and has since scored more than 30 films across Tamil, Telugu, and Hindi. He picked up two Filmfare Awards, five Vijay Awards, and nine SIIMA Awards along the way.

His Telugu run began with "Agnyaathavaasi" (2018), followed by Nani's sports drama "Jersey" (2019), whose soundtrack won him a SIIMA nomination for Best Music Director – Telugu. His biggest Telugu score to date is "Devara: Part 1" (2024), starring N.T. Rama Rao Jr. and Saif Ali Khan, which included the chart-topping single "Chuttamalle" with lyrics by Ramajogayya Sastry.
