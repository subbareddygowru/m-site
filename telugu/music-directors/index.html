Telugu Music Directors: Top Hits by Every Composer

Find the songs behind Telugu cinema's biggest soundtracks, one composer at a time. Pick a music director below to open a handpicked collection of their best Telugu songs.

Telugu film music has been shaped by a long line of composers. Ilaiyaraaja and Koti defined the classic era, Mani Sharma and Chakri powered the mass hits of the 2000s, and M. M. Keeravani took Telugu music to the world when "Naatu Naatu" from RRR won the Oscar for Best Original Song. Today Devi Sri Prasad and Thaman lead the mainstream, while Anirudh, Bheems Ceciroleo, Sai Abhyankkar and Kaala Bhairava keep bringing in fresh sounds.

Every card below links to one composer's page, with their top hits, a short profile and quick answers to common questions. You can also search the list by name.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Telugu music directors are included?

There are 29 composers here, from veterans such as Ilaiyaraaja, M. M. Keeravani and Koti to current names like Devi Sri Prasad, Thaman, Anirudh, Bheems Ceciroleo, Sai Abhyankkar and Kaala Bhairava.

Do you include composers from other film industries?

Yes, whenever they have scored Telugu films. That is why Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam composers such as AR Rahman, Anirudh, Yuvan Shankar Raja, Harris Jayaraj, B. Ajaneesh Loknath and Hesham Abdul Wahab are on the list.

How do I find a particular music director quickly?

Type the name in the search box above the cards and the list narrows as you type. You can also use the site search in the menu bar.
