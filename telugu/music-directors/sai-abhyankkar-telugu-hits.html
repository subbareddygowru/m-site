Who Is Sai Abhyankkar?

Sai Abhyankkar Ekambaresh, born on 4 November 2004 in Chennai, grew up around music by default — his parents Tippu and Harini are two of the most recorded playback voices in Tamil and Telugu cinema. He started composing on his own in his early teens, trained in vocals under Sriram Parthasarathy and A.T. Ramamoorthy, and by his own account had a bank of nearly a thousand original tunes ready before he ever signed a film.

He broke out as an independent artist rather than through a studio launch — his 2024 single "Katchi Sera" went viral and crossed 350 million YouTube views. Film offers followed fast: his debut score for the bilingual "Balti," then the Tamil romantic comedy "Dude," whose Telugu dub carried his songs into Tollywood playlists. He's since signed on to compose the full soundtrack for Atlee's Telugu-language "Raaka," starring Allu Arjun, and contributed the title track to the 2026 film "Veerabhadrudu."
