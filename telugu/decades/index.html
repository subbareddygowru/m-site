About Decades

Telugu cinema's sound has evolved dramatically across the decades. This collection lets you explore that journey era by era, from nostalgic classics to the freshest hits of today.

Telugu film music by decade

Five playlists take you from the eighties to the 2020s. The 80s and 90s lists are full of classic melodies, the 2000s collection is the largest and covers a decade of big changes, the 2010s list brings together the era when Telugu music reached audiences far beyond the region, and the 2020s playlist keeps up with what is new.

If you want a more precise look, the songs-by-year section breaks the 2010s and beyond down one year at a time.
