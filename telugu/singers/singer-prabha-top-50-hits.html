Who Is Singer Prabha?

Singer Prabha is a Telugu folk artist who sings, writes lyrics and works on music, best known for Telangana janapada songs given a modern DJ-friendly beat. The breakout was "O Pilaga Venkati" in July 2024 — Prabha wrote the lyrics and sang it, with music by Venkat Azmeera and production by Balu Paloji on the Bhavya Tunes label. It spread fast enough to get Tamil and Hindi versions in 2025, again with Balu Paloji.

The next big one was "Ranu Bombai Ki Ranu" (2025), a duet with Ramu Rathod set to music by Kalyan Keys, which has racked up tens of millions of streams. Other crowd favourites include "Nimmathota Vanamulo," "Gujjari Gujjari" and "Ringu Ringula Juttudhana," and in 2026 Prabha moved into films with "Yeshanagula" from "The Paradise." Folk fans will also find "Maa Bava Balaraju," "Naa Pere Yellamma" and "Endira Ori Venkati" in this collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Singer Prabha's most popular song?

"Ranu Bombai Ki Ranu" (2025), sung with Ramu Rathod to music by Kalyan Keys, is the biggest of the lot, with tens of millions of streams. "O Pilaga Venkati" (2024) is the song that first put Prabha on the map.

What kind of songs does Singer Prabha sing?

Mostly Telangana folk (janapada) songs, often with a DJ-style beat added, along with a few viral Telugu numbers and film singles.

Who wrote and composed "O Pilaga Venkati"?

Prabha wrote the lyrics and sang it, with music by Venkat Azmeera. It was produced by Balu Paloji and released in July 2024 on Bhavya Tunes.
