Who Is Rashmika Mandanna?

Rashmika Mandanna was born on 5 April 1996 in Virajpet, a town in Karnataka's Kodagu (Coorg) district, to Madan and Suman Mandanna. She went to Coorg Public School, then studied psychology, journalism and English literature at M.S. Ramaiah College in Bengaluru. Acting wasn't the original plan — she won the Clean & Clear Fresh Face contest in 2014, started modelling, and was noticed by Kannada filmmakers, who cast her as the lead in the romantic comedy "Kirik Party" (2016). It became a big hit and earned her the SIIMA Award for Best Debut Actress.

Her Telugu career began with "Chalo" (2018), and the same year "Geetha Govindam," opposite Vijay Deverakonda, turned her into a household name — the film earned around ₹130 crore and won her the Filmfare Critics Award for Best Actress – Telugu. Vijay and Rashmika reunited in "Dear Comrade" (2019), and she followed with "Sarileru Neekevvaru" and "Bheeshma" (both 2020) before "Pushpa: The Rise" (2021) took her across India. "Pushpa 2: The Rule" (2024), the Hindi blockbuster "Animal" (2023) and "Chhaava" (2025) followed, and Forbes India named her to its 2024 30 Under 30 list. Her next big release is "Ranabaali," a period drama with Vijay Deverakonda, with music by Ajay-Atul and a 16 October 2026 release date.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Rashmika Mandanna's first Telugu film?

"Chalo" (2018). Later that year, "Geetha Govindam" became her breakthrough and won her the Filmfare Critics Award for Best Actress – Telugu.

Which movies are featured in this playlist?

Songs from "Geetha Govindam," "Dear Comrade," "Bheeshma," "Sarileru Neekevvaru," "Pushpa," "Animal," "Ranabaali" and more of her films.

When is Ranabaali releasing, and who composed its music?

"Ranabaali," directed by Rahul Sankrityan and co-starring Vijay Deverakonda, is scheduled for 16 October 2026. Ajay-Atul composed the music, and the first single, "Endhayya Saami," is already out.
