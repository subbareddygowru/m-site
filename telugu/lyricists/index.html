About Lyricists

Great Telugu film songs are as much about the words as the music. This collection highlights playlists built around celebrated lyricists whose writing has shaped generations of listeners.

The writers behind Telugu film songs

A memorable Telugu song often lives in its lyrics. This page highlights four lyricists whose words have shaped the industry: Sirivennela Seetharama Sastry, known for thoughtful, poetic writing, Chandrabose, whose lyrics won an Oscar with Naatu Naatu, Ramajogayya Sastry, and the veteran Veturi.

If you usually pay more attention to the tune, these playlists are a nice way to listen again with the words in mind.
