Who Is Nagarjuna?

Akkineni Nagarjuna was born on 29 August 1959 in Madras (now Chennai), the youngest of five children of legendary actor Akkineni Nageswara Rao (ANR) and Annapurna. He studied mechanical engineering at the College of Engineering, Guindy, before earning a master's degree in automobile engineering in the United States — a career in engineering he set aside once acting took over. Fans and the industry know him simply as "King" Nagarjuna.

His most acclaimed early role came in "Annamayya" (1997), where he played the poet-saint Annamacharya and won a National Film Award Special Jury recognition — the film ran for 100 days in 42 centers, one of Tollywood's biggest hits of the era. Across more than 90 films, he's won nine state Nandi Awards and three Filmfare Awards South. He also co-owns MAA TV, a Telugu television channel, alongside Chiranjeevi.
