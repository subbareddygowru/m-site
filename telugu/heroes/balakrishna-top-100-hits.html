Who Is Nandamuri Balakrishna?

Nandamuri Balakrishna was born on 10 June 1960 in Chennai, the sixth son of N.T. Rama Rao — the legendary actor and three-time Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh. He made his acting debut as a 14-year-old child artist in "Tatamma Kala" (1974), which won him a Best Child Actor award, before moving into lead roles in the 1980s. Fans know him as Balayya, NBK, or Natasimham, and he holds a Bachelor's degree in Commerce from Nizam College, Hyderabad.

Across a career spanning over 100 films, he's won three Nandi Awards for Best Actor — for "Narasimha Naidu" (2001), "Simha" (2010), and "Legend" (2014) — with "Narasimha Naidu" holding the record for highest-grossing Telugu film for several years. "Gautamiputra Satakarni" (2017) marked his 100th film and won him the TSR-TV9 National Film Award for Best Actor (Telugu). He's also active in politics as an MLA for Hindupur with the Telugu Desam Party, and received the Padma Bhushan in 2025.
