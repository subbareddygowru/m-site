Trending Now: "Bhaja Govindam" from Reality

The song everyone is replaying right now is "Bhaja Govindam," the first single from Reality, Kiran Abbavaram's directorial debut. It was released in October 2026 on the Saregama Telugu label and has already crossed 5 million views on YouTube. Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu also praised it publicly, which pushed it even further.

  • Music: Bheems Ceciroleo, who also used the traditional "Bhaja Govindam" bhajan in the film's title glimpse
  • Singer: Vaikom Vijayalakshmi
  • Lyrics: Sanare
  • The feel: an upbeat song about treating life as a celebration, set against the middle-class family world of the film

In Reality, Kiran plays Anandam, a young man who manages a hotel and works to clear his family's debts. Devika Bhat is the female lead, with Sharath Kumar, Sai Kumar and Rohini in the cast. The film, produced by Sudhakar Chaganti and shot in Machilipatnam, is scheduled for release on 25 December.

Who Is Kiran Abbavaram?

Kiran Kumar Reddy Abbavaram was born on 15 July 1992 in Rayachoti, Andhra Pradesh. He came into films as an actor and writer, making his debut in the romantic comedy "Raja Vaaru Rani Gaaru" (2019), directed by Ravi Kiran Kola. The film gave him a SIIMA nomination for Best Male Debut – Telugu, and its leading lady, Rahasya Gorak, became his wife in August 2024.

He followed it with "SR Kalyanamandapam" (2021), a father-and-son drama set in Rayachoti, where he also wrote the story and screenplay, and with the 2022 releases "Vinaro Bhagyamu Vishnu Katha" and "Nenu Meeku Baaga Kavalsinavaadini," for which he wrote the screenplay and dialogue. "KA" (2024) is a mystery thriller in which he plays a dual role as Abhinaya Vasudev and KA Peddha Sir, and a sequel, "KA 2," has been announced. In 2026 he stepped behind the camera as well: "Reality," his directorial debut, is due on 25 December, and its first single, "Bhaja Govindam," has been one of the most talked-about Telugu songs of the season.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Kiran Abbavaram's first film?

"Raja Vaaru Rani Gaaru" (2019), a romantic comedy directed by Ravi Kiran Kola. His performance earned a SIIMA nomination for Best Male Debut – Telugu.

Does Kiran Abbavaram write his own films?

Yes. He wrote the story and screenplay for "SR Kalyanamandapam" (2021) and the screenplay and dialogue for "Nenu Meeku Baaga Kavalsinavaadini" (2022).

What is Kiran Abbavaram's film KA about, and is there a sequel?

"KA" (2024) is a mystery thriller in which he plays a dual role, Abhinaya Vasudev and KA Peddha Sir. A sequel, "KA 2", has been announced.

What is "Bhaja Govindam" from Reality?

"Bhaja Govindam" is the first single from "Reality," Kiran Abbavaram's directorial debut. Bheems Ceciroleo composed it, Vaikom Vijayalakshmi sang it, and Sanare wrote the lyrics. It crossed 5 million YouTube views soon after its October 2026 release.
