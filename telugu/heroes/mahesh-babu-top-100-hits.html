Who Is Mahesh Babu?

Ghattamaneni Mahesh Babu was born on 9 August 1975 in Chennai, the younger son of Telugu actor Krishna. He began as a child artist at age four in "Needa" (1979), appearing in several more films through the 1980s before taking a break to focus on his education — he holds a Bachelor of Commerce from Loyola College, Chennai. He returned as a lead actor with "Rajakumarudu" (1999), which won him the Nandi Award for Best Male Debut.

His breakthrough came with "Murari" (2001) and "Okkadu" (2003), and "Pokiri" (2006) became a genre-defining blockbuster that's still considered one of his finest performances. He's since starred in a string of commercial hits — "Dookudu," "Businessman," "Seethamma Vakitlo Sirimalle Chettu," "Srimanthudu," "Bharat Ane Nenu," "Maharshi," and "Sarkaru Vaari Paata" — earning five Filmfare Awards South and nine Nandi Awards, the most by any individual for "Srimanthudu" alone. He founded the Mahesh Babu Foundation, which funds life-saving congenital heart surgeries for children, and has featured on Forbes India's Celebrity 100 list every year from 2012 to 2025.

Pokiri (2006): The Blockbuster and Its Songs

Few films define Mahesh Babu's career like "Pokiri." Directed by Puri Jagannadh and released in April 2006, the action thriller turned him into a bona fide mass hero and went on to hold the record as the highest-grossing Telugu film of its time. It was later remade in Tamil as "Pokkiri," in Hindi, and in Kannada, which says plenty about how far its story travelled.

The music was just as big a part of the success. Mani Sharma's six-song soundtrack — "Devuda Devuda," "Dole Dole," "Gala Gala," "Ippatikinka," "Jagadame" and "Choododdu" — was a bestselling album that year. "Dole Dole" (Ranjith and Suchitra) and "Gala Gala" (Nihal) are the tracks most people still reach for first, while "Devuda Devuda" (Naveen) and "Jagadame" (Kunal Ganjawala) round out an album that mixes swagger, romance and heavy bass in equal measure.

Coming Next: Varanasi

The biggest thing on Mahesh Babu's horizon is "Varanasi," his first film with S. S. Rajamouli, the director behind "RRR." The title and a teaser were unveiled at a grand event in Hyderabad in November 2025. Mahesh plays a character called Rudhra, with Priyanka Chopra Jonas and Prithviraj Sukumaran in key roles and M. M. Keeravani composing the music. Billed as a globe-spanning action adventure shot for IMAX, it marks his first release since "Guntur Kaaram" (2024).

"Varanasi" has been officially announced for a worldwide release on 7 April 2027, timed around Ugadi. Release dates for films of this scale can move, so check the makers' official announcements for the latest. We'll keep this page updated as songs from the film arrive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Mahesh Babu's breakthrough film?

"Okkadu" (2003) marked his early breakthrough, and "Pokiri" (2006) became the genre-defining blockbuster most fans still consider his signature role.

Who composed the songs of Pokiri?

Mani Sharma composed Pokiri's six-song soundtrack, which includes "Dole Dole," "Gala Gala," "Devuda Devuda," "Ippatikinka," "Jagadame" and "Choododdu." The film was directed by Puri Jagannadh.

When does Mahesh Babu's Varanasi release?

S. S. Rajamouli's Varanasi has been officially announced for a worldwide release on 7 April 2027. As with any large film, dates can change, so check the makers' official channels for the latest.

How many Filmfare and Nandi Awards has Mahesh Babu won?

Five Filmfare Awards South and nine Nandi Awards — "Srimanthudu" (2015) alone brought him his fifth Filmfare and eighth Nandi, the most by any individual.

What charity does Mahesh Babu support?

The Mahesh Babu Foundation, which he founded to fund life-saving congenital heart surgeries for children in need.
