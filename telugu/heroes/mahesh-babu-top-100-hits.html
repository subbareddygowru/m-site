Who Is Mahesh Babu?

Ghattamaneni Mahesh Babu was born on 9 August 1975 in Chennai, the younger son of Telugu actor Krishna. He began as a child artist at age four in "Needa" (1979), appearing in several more films through the 1980s before taking a break to focus on his education — he holds a Bachelor of Commerce from Loyola College, Chennai. He returned as a lead actor with "Rajakumarudu" (1999), which won him the Nandi Award for Best Male Debut.

His breakthrough came with "Murari" (2001) and "Okkadu" (2003), and "Pokiri" (2006) became a genre-defining blockbuster that's still considered one of his finest performances. He's since starred in a string of commercial hits — "Dookudu," "Businessman," "Seethamma Vakitlo Sirimalle Chettu," "Srimanthudu," "Bharat Ane Nenu," "Maharshi," and "Sarkaru Vaari Paata" — earning five Filmfare Awards South and nine Nandi Awards, the most by any individual for "Srimanthudu" alone. He founded the Mahesh Babu Foundation, which funds life-saving congenital heart surgeries for children, and has featured on Forbes India's Celebrity 100 list every year from 2012 to 2025.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Mahesh Babu's breakthrough film?

"Okkadu" (2003) marked his early breakthrough, and "Pokiri" (2006) became the genre-defining blockbuster most fans still consider his signature role.

How many Filmfare and Nandi Awards has Mahesh Babu won?

Five Filmfare Awards South and nine Nandi Awards — "Srimanthudu" (2015) alone brought him his fifth Filmfare and eighth Nandi, the most by any individual.

What charity does Mahesh Babu support?

The Mahesh Babu Foundation, which he founded to fund life-saving congenital heart surgeries for children in need.
