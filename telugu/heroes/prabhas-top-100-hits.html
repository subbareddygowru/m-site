Prabhas

Prabhas started out doing straightforward action films through the 2000s, and Varsham (2004) was the one that actually got people talking about him. But it was S.S. Rajamouli's Baahubali duology that changed everything — the sequel became the first Indian film to cross ₹1,000 crore worldwide, and Prabhas went from a strong regional star to what people now call India's first pan-Indian superstar overnight. This playlist covers his soundtrack across that whole arc, from the early 2000s through Baahubali, Saaho, Salaar, and beyond.
