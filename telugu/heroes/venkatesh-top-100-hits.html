Who Is Victory Venkatesh?

Daggubati Venkatesh was born on 13 December 1960 in Karamchedu, Andhra Pradesh, the son of producer D. Ramanaidu. After a Bachelor of Commerce from Loyola College, Chennai, and an MBA from the Monterey Institute of International Studies, he returned to India planning to work in production — but made his acting debut instead, in "Kaliyuga Pandavulu" (1986), which won him a Nandi Special Jury Award. Fans call him Venky or Victory Venkatesh.

Over a career spanning nearly four decades and more than 70 films, he's won six Filmfare Awards South and five Nandi Awards for Best Actor, ranging across family dramas, comedies, and action films — "Swarna Kamalam," "Preminchukundam Raa," "Nuvvu Naaku Nachav," and the acclaimed 2014 remake "Drushyam" among them. He co-owns Suresh Productions with his brother D. Suresh Babu, and is part of the extended Akkineni-Daggubati family — his sister Lakshmi was married to Nagarjuna, making him Naga Chaitanya's maternal uncle.

Notable Films

  • Kaliyuga Pandavulu (1986) — his debut, which won a Nandi Special Jury Award
  • Swarna Kamalam (1988) — an early hit that showcased his versatility
  • Preminchukundam Raa (1997) — one of his most-loved romantic comedies
  • Nuvvu Naaku Nachav (2001) — a major commercial success
  • Drushyam (2014) — his acclaimed remake of the Malayalam thriller "Drishyam"
