Who Is Thalapathy Vijay?

Born Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar on 22 June 1974 in Chennai, he grew up on film sets — his father S.A. Chandrasekhar is a veteran director, and his mother Shoba Chandrasekhar is a playback singer. He appeared as a child actor in his father's film "Vetri" (1984) and studied visual communication at Loyola College, Chennai, alongside future stars Suriya, Yuvan Shankar Raja, and Karthik Raja, before his lead debut in "Naalaiya Theerpu" (1992).

Over three decades he built a soundtrack legacy running from tender 1990s melodies ("Poove Unakkaga," "Kadhalukku Mariyadhai") through his rise as a mass hero in the 2000s ("Ghilli," "Pokkiri") and into the genre-defining anthems of the 2010s and 2020s — "Thuppakki," "Kaththi," "Mersal," "Bigil," "Master," "Beast," "Varisu," and "Leo." His fans call him Thalapathy, meaning "commander," a nod to the fierce loyalty he commands both on screen and off. His 2026 film "Jana Nayagan" marked his final role as a lead actor, closing out a film career of more than 65 films as he moved into public life, founding the political party Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam in 2024.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Vijay called "Thalapathy"?

"Thalapathy" means "commander" in Tamil — the nickname reflects the fierce, near-military loyalty his fanbase has shown him throughout his career.

What was Vijay's first film?

He appeared as a child actor in his father S.A. Chandrasekhar's film "Vetri" (1984), then made his lead debut in "Naalaiya Theerpu" (1992).

What is Vijay's real name?

He was born Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar on 22 June 1974 in Chennai.

Was Jana Nayagan Vijay's last film?

Yes — "Jana Nayagan" (2026) was his final role as a lead actor, as he moved into full-time politics with the party he founded in 2024.
