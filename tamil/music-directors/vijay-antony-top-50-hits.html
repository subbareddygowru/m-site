Vijay Antony

Vijay Antony started purely as a composer before eventually stepping in front of the camera as an actor too, which is a fairly rare crossover in Tamil cinema. His music leans melodic and emotionally direct — "Naan Un" from Naan is still one of the more quietly devastating tracks to come out of Kollywood. This collects the hits from both sides of his career.
