Yuvan Shankar Raja

Son of legendary composer Ilaiyaraaja, Yuvan Shankar Raja carved his own distinct identity in Tamil cinema with a sound that fused folk, rock, electronic and hip-hop influences into something entirely fresh. Since his debut in the mid-1990s, he has become known for his raw, youthful energy, soul-stirring melodies and a knack for reinventing himself with every era of Kollywood. This collection brings together his biggest hits, moody background scores and genre-bending anthems, spanning a career built on constant experimentation.
