About Music Directors

Tamil film music has been shaped by a generation of gifted composers. This collection brings together curated Spotify playlists celebrating their biggest soundtracks.

Tamil composers worth knowing

Tamil film music has been shaped by composers across several generations. Ilaiyaraaja is the foundational name, Harris Jayaraj and Yuvan Shankar Raja defined the sound of the 2000s, D. Imman and Vijay Antony brought their own styles, and Anirudh, Sean Roldan and Sai Abhyankkar represent the newer wave.

Playing them side by side shows how the music has changed, from orchestral and folk-rooted work to the polished, beat-driven songs heard today.
