Who Is Ilaiyaraaja?

Born on 2 June 1943 in Pannaipuram, a village in Tamil Nadu's Theni district, Ilaiyaraaja grew up as Rasayya and spent his early years touring with his elder brother's musical troupe. He later moved to Madras, studied with Dhanraj Master, and earned a classical guitar qualification from Trinity College of Music, London. His film debut came with "Annakili" (1976) — a score that blended Tamil folk with Western orchestration so naturally that it changed what film music in the language could sound like, and earned him the name Ilaiyaraaja, "the young king."

What's remarkable is the sheer scale. By most counts he has scored well over a thousand films — his 1,000th was "Thaarai Thappattai" (2016) — and composed thousands of songs across Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Hindi, with Hindi credits like "Cheeni Kum," "Paa" and "Shamitabh." Fans call him Isaignani, "the musical genius," and "Maestro." He has won five National Film Awards, including for "Sindhu Bhairavi" (1985), and was honoured with the Padma Bhushan in 2010 and the Padma Vibhushan in 2018. Beyond film, he has made fusion and classical work like "How to Name It?" and the devotional "Thiruvasagam in Symphony," recorded with the Budapest Symphony Orchestra.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Ilaiyaraaja's debut film?

His debut as a film composer was "Annakili" (1976), whose folk-meets-orchestral sound made him an instant sensation in Tamil cinema.

Why is Ilaiyaraaja called Isaignani?

"Isaignani" means "musical genius" in Tamil. It's the title fans and the industry gave him, alongside "Maestro," for his scale of work and his blending of Indian folk and classical music with Western orchestration.

How many National Awards has Ilaiyaraaja won?

Ilaiyaraaja has won five National Film Awards, including Best Music Direction for "Sindhu Bhairavi" (1985). He also received the Padma Bhushan in 2010 and the Padma Vibhushan in 2018.
