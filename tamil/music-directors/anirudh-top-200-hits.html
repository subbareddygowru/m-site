Anirudh

Anirudh Ravichander made his debut on 3, still a teenager, and "Why This Kolaveri Di" went viral before viral was even the word people used for that kind of thing. Since then he's become arguably the most dominant force in Tamil film music, equally at home producing a stadium-ready mass anthem or a stripped-down acoustic number. Two hundred tracks in, and he's still setting the pace for everyone else.
